Your books stay with you

Privacy Policy

Pocket Wishes is a personal bookkeeping app with local, offline ledger features. No registration is required, and ledger content stays on your device. The app includes Google advertising and consent-management SDKs; their network processing is described separately from your books.

Keep your backup files and passwords safe. The app does not provide automatic cloud backup or cross-device synchronization. The developer has no copy of your books and cannot recover a forgotten backup password.

01. How your books are stored locally

Books, member labels, accounts, categories, income and expense entries, notes, tags, budgets, savings goals, allocation and withdrawal history, recurring plans, frequent-entry templates, receipt text you confirm, and settings are stored in the app’s local directory. They are used to display records, calculate balances and plan progress, generate statistics, and perform imports and exports you request.

The app has no developer-operated ledger server or login accounts. It does not automatically read bank or third-party payment transactions, and does not send ledger content to the developer or advertising services. Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDKs provide ads and manage consent; their network processing is explained below. Member features are only for assigning and filtering records on the same device, not online collaboration.

Local data files use iOS file protection and are excluded from automatic system backup. A local snapshot retained before a restore uses the same file protection and allows the last restore to be undone; it is not the password-encrypted backup you export separately. Device security also depends on the operating system, device passcode and usage environment.

02. Advertising services and privacy choices

The app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for banners, app-open ads, interstitials at content transitions, and rewarded videos you choose to watch. UMP collects and manages consent choices where applicable. The app initializes advertising services and requests ads only after the consent flow permits ad requests. Ad availability after declining personalization or consent depends on regional rules, your choices and SDK status; local bookkeeping remains available.

Google advertising services may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics and performance data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and maintain services. The specific data depends on the SDK, device permissions and region. Ledger amounts, income and expense records, members, notes, photos and backup files are not sent to advertising services. We do not create a separate advertising user account or developer-operated ledger analytics service.

You can reopen an available privacy form in the app under “Me → Privacy options.” Whether a form appears and which choices it offers depend on UMP and regional requirements. Where applicable, system permissions can also be managed in iOS Settings. Clearing your books does not automatically delete data Google has already processed or revoke choices made in third-party services. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising technologies information.

Rewarded videos are entirely optional. Only when the SDK confirms a reward does the app add one decorative thank-you star locally. An incomplete or unavailable video does not earn a star. Stars have no cash value and do not change account balances, budgets, savings goals or any bookkeeping data. You can continue using your books after closing a video.

03. How permissions and system services are used

FeatureWhen it is used and what it processes
Receipt photosOnly after you open the system photo picker and choose an image is that image read for on-device Apple Vision text recognition. The app does not scan the whole photo library or use cloud recognition. It does not save the original receipt image. When you check the amount and save the entry, the recognized text is stored locally with it. Recognition can be inaccurate; check before saving.
Device authenticationAfter you enable app lock, the system verifies your device passcode or available biometrics. The app does not access or store fingerprints, face templates or your passcode. The ledger is obscured in the background, and unlocking is required when you return.
NotificationsThe app requests notification permission only when you enable daily reminders. Reminders are scheduled locally, without a developer remote-push service, and do not display transaction amounts. Refusing notifications does not prevent bookkeeping.
Files and sharingOnly when you initiate an import, export or restore does the app use the system file picker or share sheet to handle the files you select. Subsequent processing by a file service depends on that service.

You can disable reminders or app lock in the app and manage related system permissions in iOS Settings. Canceling photo selection does not create a transaction. The app does not request contacts, location or microphone access.

04. Backups, exports and sharing you initiate

Encrypted backups: You create them yourself. The password you enter is used for PBKDF2 key derivation and AES-GCM authenticated encryption; it is not stored on a developer server. Save the backup in a trusted location and keep its password safely and separately. After a forgotten password, device damage, uninstall or device loss, the developer cannot recover data you have not backed up.

CSV exports: Exported CSV files are plain text and may contain amounts, account names, members, notes and tags. Anyone holding the file may read it. Check the contents and recipient before sharing. A CSV file is not an encrypted backup.

The system file picker may show your iCloud Drive or another cloud drive, and the share sheet may include third-party apps. If you choose these destinations, that service may upload or process the files under its own privacy policy. The app does not independently upload or automatically synchronize these files.

Before restoring, the app checks the file’s validity. It replaces local books only after confirmation and keeps a pre-restore local snapshot so you can undo the restore. Keep a backup you have verified works before overwriting important data.

05. Access, correction and deletion

You can view and edit entries, manage books and categories, export records and create encrypted backups in the app. To preserve accounting relationships, some deletions require you to handle related entries or withdraw money allocated to a goal first.

The clear-data action under “Me” deletes local ledger data and settings after confirmation. Copies exported to Files, cloud drives or other apps, or shared with other people, are not deleted at the same time. Manage those copies separately in their locations. The app does not delete original photos from your photo library.

There is no developer-held ledger copy to access or delete. Do not send transactions, identity documents, backup files or passwords to a public feedback channel to request deletion.

06. This website and public feedback are separate

This information website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The website itself adds no analytics tracking scripts, ads, third-party fonts, external images or CDN dependencies. Theme previews change only within the current page; the website does not create accounts or store ledger data.

When you visit, GitHub may process standard access logs, such as IP addresses, request times, device or browser information, to provide, operate and secure hosting. Its processing is governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement. This does not mean the offline app uploads transactions to GitHub.

Support is provided through GitHub Issues. Usernames, issue descriptions, comments and attachments you post there may be publicly visible, and the developer can view what you submit. Provide reproduction steps without personal information. Do not upload real transactions, account numbers, backups, passwords or unredacted screenshots. GitHub’s own terms and privacy policy apply to use of GitHub.

07. Policy changes and contact

If the app’s data processing changes, we will update this page and its date and explain the change where appropriate. This policy describes the actual behavior of the applicable app version and this website.

For questions about privacy, permissions or local data operations, see the support page or contact the maintainer through the public Issues in Jacky-LinPeng / wealth-ledger-pages. This channel is public; do not include sensitive data.
